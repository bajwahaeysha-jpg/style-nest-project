About Style.Nest

Style.Nest is a small online store built around one idea: outerwear should feel as good as it looks. We started with a single puffer jacket design and grew it into a small collection of jackets and hoodies for men, women and kids.

What we're about

Comfortable fabrics, simple designs, and prices that make sense. No unnecessary frills — just pieces you'll actually wear.

This site

This storefront was built as a front-end project — plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with the cart running on the browser's local storage. There's no real backend or payment processing behind it.
